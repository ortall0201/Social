# LinkedIn comic-style post template

Use this structure when drafting a **short, human, story-first** post (builder moment → punchline → insight), not a manifesto.

---

## Structure

**1. Opening line**  
A funny, surprising, or vivid real-life builder moment.

**2. Scene**  
What happened between Ortal and the AI / tool / agent?

**3. Tension**  
What went wrong, felt weird, or became unexpectedly funny?

**4. Insight**  
What does this reveal about working with AI, prompting, growth, or building?

**5. Positioning**  
Lightly show how Ortal thinks, builds, or solves.

**6. Ending**  
A clean last line that feels memorable, not preachy.

---

## Requirements

- 10 to 15 lines
- Simple, readable language
- Short lines
- No jargon-heavy phrasing
- No corporate tone
- No inflated futurist language
- No overly negative ranting
- Should feel like a human story, not a lecture
