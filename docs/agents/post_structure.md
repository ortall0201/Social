# LinkedIn post structure

## Format

- 10 to 15 lines
- One idea per line
- Mobile-friendly
- Visually breathable
- No dense blocks
- Easy to scan

## Default structure

1. Hook
2. Scene
3. Tension / mismatch / problem
4. What Ortal noticed
5. Insight or lesson
6. Memorable ending

## Example shape

**Line 1–2:**  
Open with a vivid or funny line

**Line 3–5:**  
Explain what happened

**Line 6–8:**  
Show the tension, weirdness, or lesson

**Line 9–12:**  
Turn it into a practical or human insight

**Line 13–15:**  
End with a clean final line that lands

## Good endings

- That is when I knew the prompt was not the problem.
- That is where the real work starts.
- Useful is better than impressive.
- Smart is not enough. It has to feel true.
- That is the difference between output and workflow.
- This is why I care so much about voice.
- The goal was never more content. The goal was better behavior.

## Do not end with

- Empty inspiration
- Fake dramatic future language
- Forced CTA in every post
- Too many hashtags
