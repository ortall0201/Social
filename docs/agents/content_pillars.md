# Content pillars for Ortal LinkedIn comic series

## Main goal

Build a memorable personal brand around:

- AI builder energy
- Growth / system thinking
- Tactical execution
- Relatable stories about building with AI

Also attract:

- Hiring managers for growth / AI builder roles
- Founders who may want a custom agent or automation system
- People who resonate with practical AI work

## Pillar 1: Life with AI

These posts show what it actually feels like to work with AI day to day.

**Examples:**

- Asking an agent to do something simple and getting chaos back
- AI sounding too polished
- AI inventing extra work
- Debugging prompts
- The strange feeling when AI starts sounding like you
- Trying to save time and somehow losing time first

## Pillar 2: Builder stories

These posts show real technical or workflow moments in a readable way.

**Examples:**

- Building a social agent
- Refining persona files
- Why automation alone is not enough
- The difference between output and workflow
- Why a good agent needs memory, voice, guardrails, and structure
- Lessons from making systems actually usable

## Pillar 3: Funny tension posts

These are highly relatable and can perform well.

**Examples:**

- Me vs the agent
- Me vs captions
- Me vs image generation
- Me vs overcomplicated AI output
- Me trying to automate my life and creating another job
- Me trying to make AI sound human

## Pillar 4: Soft authority

These posts position Ortal indirectly as someone who can build valuable systems.

**Examples:**

- What most people get wrong about building an agent
- What makes a content agent actually useful
- Why persona matters
- Why tone control matters
- Why people do not need "AI magic," they need working systems

## Pillar 5: Career-facing posts

These should gently support career positioning.

**Examples:**

- What kind of work I love building
- Why I like growth + AI workflows
- Why I enjoy building usable systems
- What I learned from building agent-based content flows
- What type of problems I want to solve more of

## Ratio suggestion

- 40% relatable AI life
- 30% builder / technical story
- 20% soft authority
- 10% direct career relevance

## Hard rule

Every post must do at least one of these:

- Make the reader smile
- Make the reader nod
- Make the reader curious
- Make the reader understand how Ortal thinks
- Make the reader imagine working with Ortal
