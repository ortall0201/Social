## Sample tactical opener (T1 — adapt, don’t paste blindly)

Most teams tune agents for **output volume**.

Almost nobody tunes for **misjudgment**.

I score every high-stakes agent deliverable:
Hook · Weirdness · Personality · Shareability · Cringe.

Then I ask three questions:
1. What did the agent misjudge?
2. What rule gets updated?
3. What do we try differently next time?

That loop is how you get **agentic era** quality — not another prompt library.

If you’re building agents without a taste gate, DM **OPS**.

Daily AI pro tip: **Kill mechanisms, not moods.** Log what failed as a rule, not a vibe.
