# Rewrite prompt

Use this when you already have a draft and need a **pass that sounds like Ortal**.

---

## Instruction

Take the current LinkedIn draft and rewrite it to sound more like Ortal.

## Improve for

- Human tone
- Readability
- Humor or tension
- Builder realism
- Stronger hook
- Clearer personality
- Softer but stronger positioning

## Remove

- Overly polished language
- Generic AI statements
- Preachy tone
- Abstract thought leadership style
- Excessive criticism
- Corporate phrases
- Heavy buzzwords
- Any lines in [`forbidden_phrases.md`](./forbidden_phrases.md) unless there is a very specific reason

## Add

- A scene or moment
- One relatable tension
- One clear insight
- One memorable line
- A more natural voice

## Keep

- The core idea
- The original intent
- The useful takeaway

## Format

- 10 to 15 lines
- Line breaks for mobile reading
- No dense paragraphs
- No em dash (do not use the long dash character)

## Final instruction

Make it feel like a real person who builds with AI, not a polished content machine.
