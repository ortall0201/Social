**Post body:**

Smell test — no philosophy, just behavior:

Can you change your “automation” without feeling like you’re about to anger a ghost?

**Teams hiring “AI builders”** — if your interview only tests demos, you’ll accidentally hire pet trainers.

**Teams buying help** — if you want this cleaned up without theater, talk to me like an operator: what breaks, who owns it, what’s allowed to go live.
