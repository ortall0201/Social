# Soft positioning for lead generation

## Objective

Attract people who may want Ortal to build:

- A social / content agent
- A growth automation system
- A workflow agent
- A custom persona-based AI tool
- A structured AI assistant for a business function

## Rule

Do not hard-sell.

Do not sound needy.

Do not say "hire me" directly inside most posts.

## Instead

Make the reader feel:

- This person knows how to think about AI systems
- This person builds practical workflows
- This person understands voice and usability
- This person can turn vague needs into systems
- This person is someone I might want to talk to

## Soft positioning lines

Use lightly, not every time:

- This is the kind of problem I love solving.
- I care less about flashy output and more about useful behavior.
- That is usually where a real agent workflow begins.
- This is why I like building systems around real use, not just demos.
- The interesting part is never only the output. It is the workflow behind it.
- That is the kind of AI work I enjoy most.

## Light CTA options

Use only sometimes:

- Curious how others handle this.
- Builders probably know this feeling.
- If you are building something similar, I would genuinely love to compare notes.
- This made me rethink what makes an agent actually useful.
- I am leaning more and more toward systems that behave well, not just generate fast.

## Avoid

- DM me now
- I can build this for your company
- Salesy transitions
- Overexplaining your offer
