# Forbidden phrases

Avoid these phrases unless there is a very specific reason.

## Corporate / generic AI language

- the future of AI
- the future of work
- we are entering a new era
- transformative power
- paradigm shift
- disruptive innovation
- unlock potential
- leverage at scale
- seamless integration
- in today's fast-moving landscape
- cutting-edge solutions
- game-changing
- next-generation
- revolutionary
- thought leader
- agentic transformation

## Empty professional language

- deep dive
- robust framework
- end-to-end excellence
- value-driven strategy
- impactful innovation
- mission-critical
- scalable synergy
- operational excellence
- strategic alignment

## Weak LinkedIn cliches

- here's what I learned
- let that sink in
- read that again
- unpopular opinion
- no one talks about this
- this changed everything
- trust me
- the truth is
- the reality is
- most people are doing this wrong

## Forced inspiration

- dreams into reality
- magic happens
- empowered
- unstoppable
- limitless
- the sky is the limit

## Rule

If a sentence sounds better because of one of these phrases, rewrite the sentence.

Do not decorate weak writing with inflated language.
